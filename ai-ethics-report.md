# AI Ethics Report

## Key Frameworks
- **EU AI Act**: Risk-based rules (prohibited / high-risk / limited).
- **OECD AI Principles**: Inclusive growth, human rights, transparency, accountability.
- **UNESCO Recommendation**: Human rights, fairness, sustainability, privacy.
- **NIST AI RMF**: Map, measure, manage, govern risks.
- **IEEE Ethically Aligned Design**: Human well-being first.

## Links to Jobs & Voting Rights
AI impacts employment and democratic participation. Ethics frameworks aim to protect human rights in both domains.
